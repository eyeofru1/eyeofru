### Layout Highlights:
1. **The Global Sidebar (Slide 1):** Features a high-contrast dark mode to clearly separate navigation from the bright workspace area. The icons are large, padded, and touch-friendly.
2. **The Client Intake Form (Slide 2):** Notice the complete absence of the sidebar here. We want the end-client to have a distraction-free, elegant experience. The dynamic checklists (Services, Products, Pages) are front and center.
3. **The Proposal Generator (Slide 3):** The live PDF canvas takes up the majority of the screen real-estate, allowing the operator to review the "Scope Overview" and the "Production Roadmap" checkboxes before hitting the primary action button to download.
